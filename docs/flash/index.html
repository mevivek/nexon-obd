Obdurate

ob·du·rate — adj. unyielding; refusing to be moved.

A read-only black box for your car. It plugs into the OBD-II port and stays there — recording every drive to onboard flash whether or not a phone is connected, printing the sample rate it is actually achieving next to every reading, and never writing anything back to the car.

  • BoardSeeed XIAO ESP32S3
  • NeedsA USB-C cable
  • CostsNothing. MIT licensed.
  • Sends homeNothing. No account, no cloud.

This browser cannot talk to serial ports. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop — Safari and Firefox have no Web Serial, and neither does any browser on iOS.

Web Serial needs a secure page. Open this over https://.

Plug the board in first. If it does not appear, hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT, and try again.

Then

  1. Join the Wi-Fi network Obdurate — password changeme1234. Change it. Anyone who can join can reflash the board.
  2. Open http://192.168.4.1.
  3. Install the dashboard once with web/deploy.sh. Until you do, the board serves a built-in fallback page with speed, rpm, coolant and battery — and it records the drive either way.

What it does on its own

  • Logs a CSV row a second to onboard flash, with empty cells where a value was not read — so a gap stays a gap and never becomes a reading of nought.
  • Reads mode 06 on-board monitor results, which show how much room a monitor has left rather than waiting for it to fail.
  • Sweeps UDS service 0x22 across the identifier space to find what your ECU actually exposes. A full pass survives the ignition being switched off and resumes where it stopped.
  • Shuts itself down below 11.8 V with the engine stopped, so it cannot flatten the battery it is plugged into.

What it will not do

It sends four services — 0x01, 0x03, 0x06 and 0x22 — and every one of them is a read. Never 0x2E (write memory), 0x31 (run a routine), 0x11 (reset an ECU), 0x10 (change session) or 0x14 (clear the car's own record).

That is a test, not a promise. The build finds every request in the source, reads its service byte, and fails if one is not on the list.

Developed against one car — a 2022 Tata Nexon 1.2 petrol. The transports, ISO-TP, the recorder and the scanner are generic; the PID list, ECU addresses and warning thresholds are that car's, and on a different one some gauges will read blank until they are changed in the source. Reading is safe regardless. If you try it on another car, the measurements are the interesting part — open an issue.
Flashing is destructive. This writes a full image at offset 0 and erases whatever was on the board, including any dashboard bundle already installed.
